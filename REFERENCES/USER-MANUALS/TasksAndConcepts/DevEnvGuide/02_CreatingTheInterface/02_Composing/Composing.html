Creating a User Interface


2 Composing the Interface


To compose your application's interface, you just drag objects off a 

palette, drop them on a window or other “surface,” and then manipulate 

and arrange them into an effective user interface. This chapter introduces 

you to Interface Builder by showing how to compose the elements of your interface.
