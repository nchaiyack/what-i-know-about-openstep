Special Tasks in the Development Environment


11 Dynamic Loading


To conserve memory, you can strategically break your project into 

pieces that are loaded on demand. For application projects, you can 

separate the interface into multiple nib files that are loaded only 

when needed. For any project, you can break out a subsystem of your 

project into a dynamically loadable bundle.
