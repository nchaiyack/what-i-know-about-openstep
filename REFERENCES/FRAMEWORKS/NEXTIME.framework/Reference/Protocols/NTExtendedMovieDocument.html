NTExtendedMovieDocument


Adopted By: NTMovieDocument class


Incorporates: NTMovieDocument protocol


Declared In: NEXTIME/NTMovieDocument.h  



Protocol Description


This protocol extends the movie-playing behavior of the NTMovieDocument protocol, which it incorporates.  Whereas the NTMovieDocument protocol provides for simple playback of movies, NTExtendedMovieDocument adds fast-forward and fast-reverse playback, as well as the ability to single-step through the movie's frames.  The actual speed of the fast-forward and fast-reverse operations are left unspecified.


Like the NTMovieDocument protocol, this protocol can be used for either software or hardware playback systems.


The NTMovieDocument class, which adopts this protocol, mutes the movie's sound during fast-forward and fast-reverse playback of the video.  Other classes conforming to this protocol can likewise mute the sound or else provide means to play it at the fast speed.

Instance Methods


fastForward:


- (BOOL)fastForward:(id)sender 


Starts fast playback in the forward direction.   If the movie is currently playing back at the normal speed (or fast in the reverse direction), playback is stopped, and the notification NTMovieDocumentDidStopPlaybackNotification posted, before attempting the fast-forward operation.


An NTMovieDocumentWillStartFastForwardNotification is posted just before beginning fast-forward playback.  During the fast-forward operation, the notification NTMovieDocumentDidSetPositionNotification is posted periodically to indicate the movie's current position.  Observers may use this notification to update their displays, reflecting the movie's current state and position.  


If the end of the movie is reached, fast forward is automatically stopped, and the notification NTMovieDocumentDidStopPlaybackNotification is posted.  Observers should update their displays accordingly.


Returns YES upon success; NO otherwise. 



fastReverse:


- (BOOL)fastReverse:(id)sender 


Starts fast playback in the backwards direction.   If the movie is currently playing back normally (or in fast-forward mode), playback is stopped, and the notification NTMovieDocumentDidStopPlaybackNotification posted, before attempting the fast-reverse operation.


This method posts the notification NTMovieDocumentWillStartFastReverseNotification just before beginning fast-reverse playback.  As with fastForward:, the NTMovieDocumentDidSetPositionNotification notification is posted periodically to indicate the movie's current position.


If the movie reaches its beginning, fast reverse is automatically stopped, and the notification NTMovieDocumentDidStopPlaybackNotification is posted to inform observers that they should update their displays.


Returns YES upon success; NO otherwise. 



stepForward:


- (BOOL)stepForward:(id)sender


Advances the current position to the next video frame and displays it.  If the movie  is currently playing, playback is stopped, and the notification NTMovieDocumentDidStopPlaybackNotification posted, before attempting to advance the movie.  This method posts the notification NTMovieDocumentDidSetPositionNotification after advancing the movie's position.  Returns YES upon success, or NO to indicate a failure (typically because the movie was already positioned at its final frame). 



stepReverse:


- (BOOL)stepReverse:(id)sender


Moves the movie's position backward one frame, displaying the new video image.  If the movie  is currently playing, playback is stopped, and the notification NTMovieDocumentDidStopPlaybackNotification posted, before attempting to step backward.  The NTMovieDocumentDidSetPositionNotification notification is posted after changing the movie's position.  Returns YES upon success, or NO to indicate a failure (typically because the movie was already positioned at the beginning).
